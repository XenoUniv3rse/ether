Five of the 20 titles failed every resolve and were retried every pass. **Fixes:** (1) grab events and outage statistics count only downloads the indexer served (`fetchNZBReporting`), not disk reads or joined downloads; (2) unattended work (preloads, prefetches, watchlist checks, detail warm-ups not promoted to Play) shares 10% of each indexer's daily API limit for searches and 10% of its daily grab limit (configured, reported `grabmax`, else 1,000) for NZB downloads, availability grabs included; playback and promoted warm-ups are never refused; (3) NZBs on disk are reused for their whole cache life, now bounded (unused 60 days, or LRU beyond 1 GiB; touched at most daily; the directory was never pruned); (4) a preloaded title whose fill fails waits 1 h, 2 h, … ≤ 24 h before it is resolved again (a settings change retries at once); a fill stopped by live playback or the disk reserve ends the pass without counting as a failure. **Measured:** lasting `TestUnattendedWorkKeepsToItsShare` (no reported limit: 150 unattended searches reach the indexer 100 times, then 20 playback and 5 promoted searches all go through; 130 unattended NZB fetches → 100 downloads; playback re-reading all 130 → 30 more downloads, 100 from disk).
