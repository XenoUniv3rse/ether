# Ether v0.3.23

Highlights:

- **Continue Watching is back for everyone.** Since 0.3.21, a Home row that only one viewer could see removed that row type for every other viewer, so Continue Watching disappeared for most of the household (#844, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **Infuse opens titles again.** Opening a film or episode in Infuse through Ether's Jellyfin connection failed with "Unexpected server response" since 0.3.21 (#845, thanks @ch1ptune).
- **Faster provider posters.** BetterPosters and other poster providers load quickly in big grids, and a poster Ether already has never waits on the provider (#846, thanks @kraigeisenman).
- **AMD Radeon hardware transcoding done right.** HDR to SDR conversion uses the GPU when that's faster, and Ether picks working paths for Radeon, Intel and NVIDIA by testing them, not by guessing.
- **Music playlists in Plex.** Plexamp and Plex Web's music Playlists tab now show and create audio playlists (#843, thanks @machetie).

## Playback and apps

- **Jellyfin and Emby** (Infuse and others):
  - every version of a title carries its file path, container, size and audio and subtitle tracks, on detail pages, playback info and extras;
  - item JSON always gets a full response, never a bare "not modified";
  - checking a playback URL (HEAD) returns the real file headers;
  - a byte-range request is never answered with a whole file after a source failover (#845).
- **Subtitles in Plex apps (and Vela's Plex mode):** a title's subtitle and audio tracks are listed from what Ether already knows when you first open it, and Ether checks the file in the background so the next look lists them all. Two subtitle tracks that look identical (same language and format) no longer share one ID, so every track can be picked.
- **Plugin titles that kept saying "still being prepared"** now play: retries join the one search already running and reuse its results instead of starting it over, and the request waits until a playable source is found. While Ether is still searching, apps see "Finding a source…", and while a download service is fetching an uncached release, "Waiting for the download service…" with its queue position, both with a retry hint.
- **Seeking right after starting** no longer fails with a server error: a segment request that races the restart is served from the new stream or asked to retry.
- **Plex Companion:** players that relay remote control through their server, such as Plex for Samsung TVs, can now be cast to and controlled through Ether from the phone app, Plex Web's "Play on" and scripts. Each viewer reaches only their own players (#837, thanks @jmiller0).
- **Chapter skip in the Jellyfin Roku app:** the chapter back and next buttons beside Play/Pause now work. Jellyfin and Emby get the real chapters of the version being played, and Jellyfin Web keeps showing chapter covers (thanks @krakenn9).
- **Collections rows on Home:** a Home row of collection cards no longer stops Vela's Home from loading. Vela shows the cards natively and each opens its collection, and a Home row that can't be built is left out instead of failing the whole page (#847, thanks @Motley-Brew).
- **Music playlists in Plex:** audio playlists list, play and can be created from tracks, with add, remove and reorder. A music plugin's list levels appear as read-only playlists, and Jellyfin and Emby show the same playlists as audio (#843).

- **Shows and films with a future year in their name** ("Blade Runner 2099") play again: Ether read "2099" in a release name as its release year, so it saw "Blade Runner" and rejected every release as a title mismatch. A year later than next year is now always part of the title.

- **Music that won't play says why:** when an album can't play because an indexer's daily API or download limit ran out, or every release was turned down, playback and Releases › History now say so instead of "not available yet".

## Live TV

- **Guide covers for films and shows:** a guide programme that comes without an image (no XMLTV icon) gets its poster from Ether's catalogue when Ether can identify it, matched once at guide import. Ether's own virtual channels show each title's artwork too. This works in Vela, Plex, Jellyfin/Emby and Xtream.

- **Catch-up library** (thanks @kraigeisenman):
  - Plex apps show catch-up artwork (#839);
  - an airing without an episode title is named by when it aired, in your language ("Wed Nov 25, 6:30 PM"), instead of repeating the show name (#840);
  - the library sorts by title, newest or oldest, in Plex, Jellyfin/Emby and Silo (#841);
  - search finds catch-up airings: Plex adds a Catch-up hub after On TV (#842).

## Home and rows

- **Plex follows a library's tabs:** hiding a library's **Library** tab (Libraries › the library › Tabs) now also removes Plex's Library tab, and the section opens on the first visible tab, usually Recommendations. Plex also follows the order and visibility of Recommendations and Collections, as Vela already did. Search, collections and direct links keep working (#850, thanks @Motley-Brew).

- **The Watchlist row uses its name:** it's one row, newest first, titled with the row's name ("Watchlist" by default), with titles that can't play yet marked "Not yet available" in apps that show badges. A new row option, **Split by availability**, brings back the separate "Waiting for a stream" and "Now available" rows. A title added from any app is checked straight away, whether or not Prewarm is on, so a playable title no longer sits under "Waiting for a stream" for half an hour (#848, thanks @Motley-Brew).

- **Collections pages load fast in big libraries:** a page of collections in Vela, Jellyfin and Emby reads only that page's collections, instead of probing every collection in the library one at a time (760 database statements down to about 20 on a 300-collection test library; thanks @krakenn9).

- **Rows with an audience** only take over their row type for the viewers in that audience. Everyone else gets Ether's normal row, on Home and every library's Recommendations page. Existing pages heal when they load, with nothing to change (#844).

## Transcoding

- **AMD Radeon (VAAPI):**
  - **HDR to SDR** on the GPU through Vulkan (libplacebo) when the Radeon driver has no VAAPI tone-mapping. Ether measures the CPU and GPU routes once, then picks the faster one per stream, moving to the GPU when the CPU is busy, and declines a stream that neither route can keep up with;
  - if the GPU ever reports a lost device, Ether stops using that route on that GPU until restart and falls back to the CPU;
  - hardware deinterlacing is only used once Ether has checked it actually removes combing;
  - subtitle burn-in works where the driver lacks `overlay_vaapi`;
  - AV1 hardware decoding is detected correctly;
  - 10-bit HEVC and the bitrate cap are probed and respected.
- **Hardware probing:** Intel Quick Sync is only probed on Intel GPUs, expected probe failures aren't logged as warnings, and Ether uses the GPU that passes an encode test when there are several. The Playback page shows the route chosen for the latest HDR conversion.

## Posters and artwork

- **Provider posters** (BetterPosters, PostersPlus, XRDB, RPDB, PosterFlow and custom templates):
  - several are fetched at once per provider, instead of two for the whole server;
  - a stored poster is served at once and refreshed in the background;
  - waiting for a fetch slot no longer counts against the timeout, so busy grids don't fall back to TMDB art (#846).
- **Artwork at the size apps ask for:** Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Vela, Silo, Stremio and Xtream clients get posters, backdrops, logos and thumbnails scaled to the requested size, never upscaled, and cached once per size. A 640-pixel backdrop is now about 4 KB, where it used to be the 80 KB original.

## Console

- **Stremio install link behind a subpath:** with a public URL that has a path (for example `https://host/media`), the Stremio install link, QR code and `stremio://` link now include `/stremio/` before the key, and posters and streams use the same address (#852, thanks @steelricky).

- **Record a problem always lets you finish:** when the app with the problem never reached Ether (nothing loads at all), the recording can still be finished and downloaded, with the server's side of the evidence and "Device not identified". The panel says what Finish is waiting for.

- **Trusted proxies by name** also work when the system resolver can't see Docker container names (for example behind a Cloudflare WARP container): Ether asks Docker's own DNS, only when it's running inside Docker. Entries may be written as URLs (#835, thanks @EsmailELBoBDev2).

## Viewer portal

- **Invite links:** an invite link only invites. Accepting asks for a username and password straight away, so closing the tab can't lock a new viewer out, or they can use their Plex account instead. A used or expired link says so. The TV-code screen says which account is signed in. Jellyfin's sign-in page gives a hint about usernames and Quick Connect without revealing any (#834, thanks @EsmailELBoBDev2).

## Plugins

- **Plugin libraries filtered by an empty field** come back fast on big libraries: Ether keeps a running count of which items have each field, and when few items match it finds those first instead of checking items one by one in sort order. A rare empty value on a million-item PostgreSQL library no longer times out (#849, thanks @FizzWhirl).

## Performance

- **Vela Home opens faster on big libraries:** a Home visit reads a small, fixed amount from the database (about a tenth of the queries and a twentieth of the rows on a large test library), and library rows pages read far less. The Live TV row reads only the programmes it shows.
- **Right after Ether restarts,** Vela's first Home load no longer fails with "Your library couldn't be reached": an unchanged collection refresh no longer looks like a permission change, and Home rebuilds itself once if permissions really do change while it loads. That first load also no longer waits on unrelated virtual channels.
- **Live TV in Vela** keeps its channel cards for the life of the stream instead of re-reading the whole lineup on every screen update. The channel list, the Sports screen and live-event overlays match events to channels once and read the guide in batches (the Sports screen went from about 1,600 database queries to about 100 on a 300-channel test lineup).
- **Plex Home and library hubs** fetch their titles' details, watch state and resume points in one batch per page, and no longer re-read plugin settings for every item.

## Plugins (developers)

- **Faster plugin downloads:** `http.fetchToCache` takes a `connections` option (1–8, for jobs and background calls). When the server supports ranges and gives a validator, Ether fetches parts at once and writes each at its offset, then checks the whole file. `resume` keeps each part's progress. A server without range support falls back to one connection (#851, thanks @FizzWhirl).

- **Kind resolvers can see the owner's debrid policy:** `sources.resolve({kind: "debrid"})` answers `{enabled, cachedOnly}`, and `debrid_check` includes `cachedOnly`. A resolve reply may carry both `sources` and `pending`: Ether tries the sources first, queues an uncached torrent when the owner allows it, and otherwise keeps the viewer waiting on the plugin's own preparation (#853, thanks @FizzWhirl).

## Reliability

- **Flows:** reloading flows no longer races a running flow's result.

## Upgrade notes

- **Vela 0.2.77** or later is recommended.
